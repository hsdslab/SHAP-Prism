# Data licenses and source terms

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers SHAP Prism software and original project
documentation. It does not relicense third-party datasets.

This repository contains no empirical dataset and no empirical raw, cleaned,
transformed, prediction, or explanation table. Its examples, tests, and
documentation use code-defined synthetic values. Those project-authored
synthetic examples are covered by the repository's MIT license.

The article applications refer to Minnesota barley, Airfoil Self-Noise,
wave-soldering, and Palmer Penguins data, but no copy or row-level derivative
of any of them is part of this payload. Obtain any source from its provider and
review the provider's current terms. Official acquisition links and the
external-input boundary are documented in `ARTICLE_DATA_ACQUISITION.md`.
